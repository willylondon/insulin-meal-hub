    About & privacy

    What this app is: a collection of Jamaican-style recipe ideas and a simple meal log. It is not a medical device, gives no medical advice and does not calculate insulin or medication doses.

    Recipes: not yet reviewed by a dietitian, clinician or food-safety specialist. Nutrition figures are withheld until they have been checked. Follow the cooking temperatures in each recipe and use a food thermometer.

    Your data: your meal log, notes and settings are stored only in this browser on this device. Anyone using this browser profile can see them. "Reset All Data" deletes them from this device.

    AI generator: only when you use it, your selected proteins, meal type and diet settings are sent to our server (hosted on Vercel) and to Anthropic to write a recipe. Your log and notes are never sent. The providers may keep request logs under their own policies.
